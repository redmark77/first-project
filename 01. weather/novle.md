# The Umbrella

Mia lived in a small town where it rained almost every day. She did not like the rain. It made her shoes wet and her hair messy.

One morning, a strange old man stood at the bus stop. He held a bright red umbrella with golden stars on it.

"Would you like to borrow my umbrella?" he asked.

Mia looked at the sky. Dark clouds were coming. "Yes, thank you," she said.

She opened the umbrella. Suddenly, the raindrops turned into music. Each drop made a soft, sweet sound, like a tiny bell. People on the street stopped and smiled. A little dog danced in a puddle. An old woman began to sing.

Mia laughed. For the first time, she did not mind the rain at all.

When she reached home, she wanted to return the umbrella. She ran back to the bus stop, but the old man was gone. Only a small note lay on the bench.

*"Keep it. The rain sounds better when you share it with others."*

From that day, Mia carried the red umbrella everywhere. Whenever it rained, she opened it for anyone standing nearby — a tired worker, a crying child, a lost traveler. And every single time, the rain turned into music.

Years later, people in the town still talked about the girl with the singing umbrella. No one knew where she found it. But everyone agreed on one thing:

Rainy days were never sad, as long as Mia was around.

**The End.**
